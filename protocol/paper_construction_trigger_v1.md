# Paper Construction Trigger v1

Paper construction proceeds in two synchronized phases so writing does not become the critical path.

## Phase A — begin now
The following sections may be developed from frozen concepts, protocols, and verified literature:
- title and contribution framing;
- Introduction;
- closest-prior-work positioning;
- research questions;
- SCDA conceptual architecture;
- review/search/screening methodology;
- coding architecture and nested-model hypotheses/tests;
- reliability, holdout, robustness, provenance, and falsification methods.

No unfrozen corpus count or architecture-performance conclusion is written as a final empirical claim.

## Phase B — triggered by evidence freeze
Final empirical Results, Discussion, managerial implications, limitations tied to observed results, and Conclusion begin only after:
1. Stage-2 eligibility is frozen;
2. primary and review/framework layers are separated;
3. claim-level coding is complete;
4. genuine independent coder-B reliability is measured and adjudicated as required;
5. comparator mappings are frozen;
6. K4/KS7/KSV8/SCDA9, ablation, holdout, V/I, robustness and managerial analyses complete;
7. headline artifacts and provenance are frozen.

The evidence, not the intended narrative, determines whether the final representation is K4, KS7, KSV8, SCDA9, or a qualified/orthogonal-layer interpretation.

## Final submission barrier
After Phase B, populate claim provenance and the paper claim map, verify references and closest prior work, then run G13-G20 and create the blinded IEEE TEM package.
