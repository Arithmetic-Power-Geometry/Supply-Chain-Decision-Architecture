# Evidence-Layer Matched-Case Falsification Protocol v1

## Question
Does evidence maturity V preserve useful information after the underlying decision and solution are held approximately constant?

## Frozen evidence levels
conceptual; synthetic; simulation; benchmark; case study; observational; pilot; deployed; longitudinal.

No additional evidence label may be introduced after results are inspected without creating a new protocol version.

## Matching
Primary exact-match key:
decision level + process + flow + objective + theory + method + technology.

A matched group requires at least two claims. Prefer groups spanning at least two independent publications. Multiple claims from one publication are retained for provenance but do not by themselves establish cross-study evidence progression.

## Primary diagnostic
For each matched group report:
- claim count;
- independent study count;
- observed evidence levels;
- number of distinct evidence levels;
- ordinal rank span.

Report the proportion of matched groups in which V varies while K and S remain matched.

## Interpretation
Variation in V demonstrates incremental descriptive information that K/S do not encode. It does not demonstrate causal validity, superior managerial performance or that later evidence levels are universally better.

No variation in V means this test provides no support for V as an identity-defining dimension.

## Robustness
Repeat with progressively relaxed matching only through prespecified rules, reported separately from exact matching. Relaxation must not be chosen because it creates favorable V variation.

## Architecture consequence
If V is reliably codable and repeatedly separates otherwise matched K/S cases across independent studies and holdouts, KSV8 remains a serious candidate.
If V is useful primarily for assessing claim support but not for distinguishing decision/solution identities, report it as an orthogonal evidence annotation rather than forcing it into the core architecture.
If V is unreliable and non-informative, demote or remove it.

The architecture follows the evidence.
