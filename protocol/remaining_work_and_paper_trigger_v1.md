## Paper trigger

Paper architecture, introduction, theory/background and methods may be developed before Step 20 if they contain no unfrozen empirical claims.

The empirical Results, Discussion, managerial implications and Conclusion are written only after Steps 1-20 are complete and frozen.

No architecture is declared the winner in advance. If KS7 or KSV8 is the smallest adequate representation, the paper reports that result rather than forcing SCDA9.

## Submission trigger

Submission-ready status requires completion of Step 25 and no blocking G13-G20 findings. Passing internal gates reduces avoidable errors; it does not guarantee editorial acceptance.
