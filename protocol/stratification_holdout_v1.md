# Stratification and Holdout Plan v1

## Purpose
Prevent convenience sampling and evaluate whether SCDA behavior generalizes beyond the records used to refine coding rules.

## Era strata
pre-2005; 2005--2014; 2015--2019; 2020--2023; 2024--2026.

## Analytical strata
Preserve representation across management/organization, operations research/optimization, industrial engineering/operations, information systems/digital technology, and AI/data-science work where source metadata permits defensible assignment.

## Decision strata
Strategic, tactical, operational and real-time/autonomous; Plan, Source, Make, Store, Move, Deliver, Return, Recover and Enable.

## Evidence strata
Conceptual, synthetic, simulation, benchmark, case study, observational, pilot, deployed and longitudinal.

## Holdout
Before final model comparison, reserve a deterministic holdout defined from stable record identifiers and stratified by era. Coding definitions may be clarified using the development subset, but architecture claims are evaluated again on the untouched holdout.

## Cluster rule
Claims from the same publication stay in the same development/holdout partition to prevent leakage.

## Reporting
Report corpus counts by era, discipline, decision level, process, evidence maturity and industry/context. Empty or sparse strata are reported rather than filled opportunistically.

## Sensitivity
Repeat headline metrics after excluding ambiguous codes and after collapsing closely related categories. A conclusion is robust only if its direction survives these prespecified alternatives.
