## Scientific interpretation: protocol deviation to disclose

The protocol requires revise/merge/demote/remove for dimensions with kappa <0.67 before headline architecture claims. Eight of nine fail; only technology passes. Keeping nine dimensions for *exploratory collision comparisons* is analytically possible, but it does **not** satisfy the original development gate. A manuscript correction should:

1. Explicitly disclose the original <0.67 gate and the deviation, rather than describing weak reliability merely as a limitation.
2. Report the study-level 125-pair calculation and the treatment of blank `theory` values as a category; distinguish it from the claim-level non-missing-pair protocol.
3. Identify SCDA9 as the conditional winner for the specified collision rule, **not** a validated nine-dimensional observer-independent taxonomy.
4. Separate the 124-claim primary analysis from the 125-study paired-coder stress test.
5. Retain the failed temporal holdout and coder sensitivity, without overstating predictive or managerial utility.

### Proposed concise text for manuscript (subject to authors' approval)

> The study-level paired-coder analysis reproduced the reported agreement statistics, but eight of nine dimensions fell below the protocol's internal development threshold of 0.67. Retaining these dimensions for exploratory architecture-comparison experiments therefore constitutes a departure from the prespecified gate and must not be interpreted as validation of a stable nine-dimensional classification instrument. The 125-study paired exercise is distinct from the frozen 124-claim primary analysis. In the paired exercise, blank theory values were counted as an explicit category, whereas the earlier claim-level reliability protocol specified matched non-missing claims. SCDA9 is consequently reported as the preferred representation under the stated collision rule, not as a reliably codable or prospectively superior managerial decision framework.

This text should not be used to imply that historical independence or pre-adjudication timing has been independently established.
