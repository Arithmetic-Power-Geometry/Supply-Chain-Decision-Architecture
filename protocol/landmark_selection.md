# Landmark Study Selection Protocol

The landmark set is not the systematic-review corpus and must not be treated as a statistically representative sample.

## Purpose
1. Anchor historical eras.
2. Stress-test SCDA on influential studies with very different epistemic forms.
3. Prevent a present-day AI/digital framing from distorting foundational SCM concepts.
4. Establish citation-chaining seeds for the systematic corpus.

## Admission criteria
A landmark candidate should satisfy at least two:
- introduced or consolidated a durable SCM construct;
- materially changed how a major SCM decision problem is formulated;
- established an influential empirical consequence;
- created a framework repeatedly used by later SCM research;
- anchors a distinct methodological or technological transition.

Bibliographic identity must be verified from a publisher, DOI registry, professional society, or similarly authoritative source.

## Coding rule
Landmark records are coded only after the full abstract and, where necessary, full text are inspected. Historical papers are not assigned technologies or theories that they did not actually use.

## Separation from evidence corpus
Landmarks guide historical interpretation and citation chaining. Quantitative claims about SCDA performance will be computed only on the frozen systematic corpus.
