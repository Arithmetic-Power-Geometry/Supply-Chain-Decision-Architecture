# Paper Claim and Section Alignment Protocol v1

## Purpose
The final manuscript is written from validated evidence artifacts, not from a preselected narrative. Every headline result must be registered before final prose is frozen.

## Claim map
Each registered claim records:
- stable claim_id;
- claim class;
- manuscript section;
- provisional claim template;
- artifact path and exact statistic/field;
- evidence scope;
- maximum permitted wording strength;
- status.

## Wording hierarchy
Descriptive evidence permits descriptive language.
Comparative analysis permits statements about observed differences under the specified analysis.
Reliability evidence permits statements about reproducibility only for the coded dimensions and sample evaluated.
Holdout evidence permits limited generalization only to represented periods/disciplines.
Managerial ambiguity diagnostics permit descriptive/comparative usefulness claims, not causal performance claims.
Methodological artifacts permit statements about procedure/reproducibility, not substantive SCM effects.

Words implying causation, effectiveness, superiority, universality, prediction, deployment benefit or managerial performance require evidence specifically designed to support them.

## Section consistency
The same empirical conclusion must retain the same scope in Abstract, Results, Discussion, Managerial Relevance Statement and Conclusion. The abstract/conclusion cannot upgrade a qualified Results statement.

## Negative and null findings
Null, unstable or falsifying results are retained. If KS7 is smallest adequate, the paper cannot describe SCDA9 as empirically necessary. If V or I functions better as an annotation, the title/abstract/conclusion must reflect that result.

## Status
planned: intended analysis, not yet evidentially supported.
pending: artifact exists or is expected but support is not frozen.
supported: exact artifact/statistic verified.
narrow: original wording must be reduced to supported scope.
remove: claim must not appear in the final paper.

Only supported claims may become headline results.

## Final manuscript audit
After the post-G20 rewrite, map each headline sentence in Abstract, Results, Discussion, Managerial Relevance and Conclusion back to this ledger. Any unmapped substantive result is either registered and audited or removed.
