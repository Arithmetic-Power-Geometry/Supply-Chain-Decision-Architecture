# Stage-1 Screening Execution v1

The Stage-1 ledger is initialized mechanically from the stable candidate corpus. Initialization makes no inclusion or exclusion judgment.

Each record is reviewed on title and available abstract under the frozen two-stage screening codebook. Permitted Stage-1 decisions are include, exclude, or uncertain.

An absent abstract is not itself an exclusion criterion. When the title/available metadata cannot support a defensible decision, mark uncertain and route the record to additional evidence/adjudication.

Exclusions require an applicable frozen E01--E10 reason. E10 requires a note. Reviewers must not target a desired final corpus size.

Automated keyword rules may be used for navigation or prioritization only; they cannot be recorded as substantive reviewer decisions.

The completed ledger must contain exactly one Stage-1 decision for every stable candidate record before Stage 1 is frozen.
