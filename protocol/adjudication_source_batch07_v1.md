# Stage-1 Source Verification — Batch 07

Twenty queued records were source-adjudicated.

Outcome: 17 include, 3 exclude, 11 primary, 6 review/synthesis, 0 unresolved.

Publisher/DOI evidence was preferred, with PubMed/PMC or institutional repositories used where they exposed stronger method detail.

Scope controls:
- AI/digitalisation for sustainable-development research is E02 because SCM is not its substantive research object.
- The building CO2 digital-twin study is E02 because its object is building emissions.
- The generic Digital Twin-IoT-blockchain architecture article is E02 because its object is communications/computing architecture rather than SCM.

Explicit systematic/state-of-the-art reviews remain in the review layer. Empirical surveys, optimization/network models, decision frameworks, intelligent systems, and substantive conceptual SCM contributions are primary.

Reviewer-A source adjudication remains separate from independent second-coder reliability and does not satisfy G4.
