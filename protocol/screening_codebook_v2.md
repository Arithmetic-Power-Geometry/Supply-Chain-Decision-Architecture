# Screening Codebook v2

## Unit
Screen at publication level before claim-level coding.

## Include in primary layer
Peer-reviewed scholarly work materially examining a supply-chain phenomenon, decision, coordination mechanism, model, capability, technology or outcome, with sufficient source information for at least the Decision Kernel.

## Review layer
Systematic, scoping, bibliometric, critical and framework reviews are retained separately for intellectual history, comparator construction and citation chaining. They are not mixed with primary-study prevalence estimates.

## Exclusion reasons
E01 duplicate;
E02 not supply-chain relevant;
E03 no substantive decision/phenomenon;
E04 editorial/news/promotional/non-scholarly;
E05 insufficient verifiable metadata;
E06 non-English where eligibility cannot be determined;
E07 review/framework routed to review layer;
E08 inaccessible evidence needed for coding;
E09 outside frozen date boundary;
E10 other, with mandatory note.

## Decisions
include_primary; include_review; exclude; uncertain.

Uncertain records require adjudication and cannot silently enter quantitative analysis.

## Audit rule
Every deduplicated candidate must have exactly one terminal screening decision before corpus freeze.
