# Manuscript Production Gates v1

## G15 Mechanics
The submission source must contain no unresolved ?? markers, TODO/TBD/FIXME/PLACEHOLDER text, duplicate labels, references to missing labels, or figure/table/algorithm floats without captions and labels. Unused labels are reported for review.

## G16 Double-blind
The blinded manuscript must be screened for author names, institutional identity, personal email addresses, acknowledgments and repository/identifier strings that reveal authorship. Automated detection is a minimum check; manual inspection of PDF metadata, acknowledgments, supplementary files and self-citation wording is mandatory.

A public repository may be cited in the author-identifiable archival version, but an identifying repository URL must not silently defeat double-blind review.

## G17 Front matter
Project submission gates:
- abstract: 200--250 words;
- Managerial Relevance Statement: 150--200 words.

These limits are rechecked against the journal's live author instructions immediately before submission.

## Production order
Do not finalize prose before the evidence corpus and G1--G14 artifacts are frozen. After G20, rebuild the submission paper from the validated evidence package, then rerun G13--G20 on the final source/PDF. A gate failure after rewriting requires correction and another audit cycle.
