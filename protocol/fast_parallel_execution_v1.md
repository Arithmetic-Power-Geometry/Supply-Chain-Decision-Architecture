# Fast Parallel Execution Plan v1

The objective is to shorten calendar time without weakening review validity or allowing downstream results to contaminate upstream decisions.

## Parallel lanes

### Lane A — Stage-2 evidence acquisition
Run deterministic acquisition batches independently. Record source availability and provenance only. Acquisition failure is not an eligibility exclusion.

### Lane B — Stage-2 eligibility
Screen records whose source evidence is available. Eligibility criteria remain frozen. Uncertain records enter adjudication; they are not forced to include/exclude for speed.

### Lane C — Comparator preparation
Encode genuine literature frameworks and mappings independently of SCDA outcome analysis. Freeze comparator definitions before headline model competition.

### Lane D — Claim-coding infrastructure
Prepare normalized claim-level schemas, validation rules, controlled vocabularies, and coding examples. Do not populate unsupported claims before eligible source evidence is available.

### Lane E — Reliability preparation
Preselect the stratified reliability-sampling procedure and coder-B package. Actual coder-B judgments must remain genuinely independent and cannot be simulated or AI-filled.

### Lane F — Paper-safe writing
Develop title options, introduction, motivation, closest-prior-work structure, research questions, conceptual architecture and methods from already frozen protocol material. Do not write final empirical Results, Discussion, managerial implications or Conclusion before evidence/results freeze.

### Lane G — analysis automation
Keep model competition, ablation, holdout, evidence/context, robustness and managerial-value pipelines ready so they execute immediately after claim-level corpus freeze.

## Dependency barrier

Stage-2 final corpus -> claim-level coding -> reliability/coding freeze -> model/comparator analyses -> results freeze -> full results-driven paper.

No parallel lane may bypass this dependency barrier.

## Fast-paper trigger

The paper shell and evidence-stable Sections I-IV may advance now. The complete paper begins immediately after frozen claim-level evidence and required reliability permit the full analysis battery to run. Final Results/Discussion/Conclusion are generated only from those frozen outputs.
