# Layered Supply Chain Decision Architecture (SCDA)

## Motivation
The nine coding dimensions are not treated as nine interchangeable coordinates. They represent different explanatory roles and are organized into four layers.

## 1. Decision Kernel K
K = (L, P, F, O)

- L — decision level
- P — supply-chain process
- F — principal flow affected
- O — decision objective

The kernel answers: **What decision is being made, where in the supply chain, over what flow, and toward what objective?**

## 2. Solution Layer S
S = (T, M, G)

- T — theoretical lens
- M — analytical/empirical/computational method
- G — enabling technology

The solution layer answers: **How is the decision explained, studied, or enabled?**

Technology is denoted G here to avoid confusing it with evidence.

## 3. Evidence Layer V
V records the maturity of evidence supporting the contribution, from conceptual through longitudinal/deployed evidence.

It answers: **How strongly has the proposed relationship or capability been demonstrated?**

## 4. Context Layer I
I records industry/application context.

It answers: **Where has the contribution been studied or demonstrated?**

## Complete representation
R = (K, S, V, I)
  = ((L,P,F,O),(T,M,G),V,I)

## Causal/interpretive ordering
Problem context
→ Decision Kernel
→ explanatory/solution mechanism
→ enabling technology
→ evidence
→ application context/outcomes

The ordering is interpretive, not a claim that every study implements a causal chain.

## Design principles
1. Decision identity must not be defined by the technology used to solve it.
2. Method must not be conflated with managerial theory.
3. Technological novelty must not imply evidence maturity.
4. Application context must not determine decision identity.
5. The full representation is retained only if real-corpus evaluation shows useful non-redundant information beyond the kernel.

## Competing representations to test
K4: L + P + F + O
KS7: K4 + T + M + G
KSV8: KS7 + V
SCDA9: KSV8 + I

The empirical study will determine whether SCDA9 is justified or whether a more parsimonious representation should be preferred.
