# Evidence Maturity Coding Amendment v1

## Reason
Primary-study calibration showed that study method and evidence maturity can be conflated if a paper combines analytical modelling, numerical experiments, and empirical observations.

## Rule
Method answers: **How is the research conducted?**

Evidence maturity answers: **What is the strongest form of evidence actually demonstrated for the focal claim?**

These fields must be coded independently.

A theoretical model with numerical experiments is not automatically empirical evidence. A paper that also validates a focal claim against observed real-world data may receive an empirical evidence code only when that validation is explicit and relevant to the coded claim.

Where a study contains multiple evidence forms, preserve them in provenance notes. The single evidence-maturity field records the strongest evidence relevant to the focal coded claim, not the most sophisticated technique used anywhere in the paper.

## Calibration consequence
P001 and P002 demonstrate why method and evidence cannot be inferred from one another. Their final evidence labels remain subject to claim-level full-text coding.

No quantitative pilot comparison is considered validated until this rule is applied consistently to all pilot records.
