# Two-Stage Eligibility Screening v3

## Stage 1 — Title and abstract
Each deduplicated candidate is screened for central relevance to supply-chain management and substantive decision, coordination, model, capability, technology or outcome content.

Decisions: include; exclude; uncertain.

An Stage-1 inclusion means only that the record advances to full-text assessment. It is not inclusion in the analytical corpus.

## Stage 2 — Full text
Full text is assessed against the same substantive scope and the availability of sufficient source evidence for defensible coding. Included studies are typed as primary or review/framework. Reviews are retained in a separate synthesis/comparator layer and do not enter primary-study prevalence estimates.

## Exclusion codes
E01 duplicate
E02 not supply-chain relevant
E03 no substantive decision/phenomenon
E04 editorial/news/promotional/non-scholarly
E05 insufficient verifiable metadata
E06 language prevents eligibility determination
E07 review/framework routed from primary layer
E08 evidence inaccessible for required coding
E09 outside frozen date boundary
E10 other, with mandatory explanatory note

## Adjudication
Uncertain records cannot enter the frozen corpus. They require adjudication. Screening disagreements are preserved rather than overwritten when independent screening is introduced.

## Flow accounting
Identification, title/abstract screening, full-text assessment, exclusions by reason, and final primary/review inclusions are generated from the ledger by code. Counts must never be manually typed into the paper.

## Reporting constraint
Use PRISMA terminology only for steps actually executed and documented. OpenAlex discovery must not be described as Scopus or Web of Science retrieval.
