# Bibliography and Screened-Corpus Policy v1

The systematic-review evidence base and the manuscript bibliography are related but are not identical objects.

The evidence repository preserves complete bibliographic identity, screening disposition, and available provenance for every one of the 659 Stage-2 screened records. The frozen screening accounting is:

659 screened = 518 included + 141 excluded.

Excluded records remain in the evidence trail because transparent review accounting requires their disposition to be reproducible. Their presence in the repository does not make them manuscript references.

Included records also have different analytical roles. Primary studies support claim-level empirical coding and architecture evaluation. Reviews, synthesis papers, and framework papers support historical synthesis, prior-framework reconstruction, comparator design, and interpretation where appropriate.

The manuscript bibliography contains every source that is legitimately cited in the paper's review, synthesis, methodology, framework comparison, empirical analysis, discussion, or other scholarly argument. No source is inserted merely to make the bibliography numerically equal to the screened corpus.

Accordingly, manuscript production must never apply the instruction "cite all 659 references." The correct requirement is:

"Preserve complete bibliographic details and disposition for all 659 screened records in the evidence repository, while the manuscript bibliography contains every source legitimately cited by the review, synthesis, methodology, comparisons, and empirical analysis."

A screened-record count is a review-flow quantity, not a bibliography target.
