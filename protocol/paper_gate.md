# Paper Readiness Gate

The full paper should be drafted only after all mandatory gates pass.

## Gate A — Concept
- Definitions frozen
- Novelty claim survives review-of-reviews
- Relationship to prior taxonomies explicitly documented

## Gate B — Corpus
- Search strategy documented
- Review-of-reviews corpus substantially complete
- Primary corpus large and stratified enough for planned analyses
- Deduplication and screening log complete

## Gate C — Coding
- Codebook frozen
- Reliability sample independently recoded
- Agreement/kappa reported
- Ambiguous records flagged

## Gate D — Computation
- Genuine literature baselines implemented
- Ablation analysis complete
- Robustness/sensitivity analysis complete
- All headline results regenerated by workflow

## Gate E — Evidence
- Tables and figures generated from frozen artifacts
- Every quantitative claim traceable to an artifact
- No synthetic benchmark used as evidence about the literature
- Limitations documented

## Gate F — TEM fit
- Clear engineering-management theoretical contribution
- Clear managerial implications
- Managerial Relevance Statement drafted
- Review contributes new framework/taxonomy and new insights, not only summary
- Initial manuscript follows current IEEE-TEM submission requirements

## Writing point
Write the full manuscript after Gates A-E pass. Before then, maintain only a living outline, research questions, methods skeleton, and evidence ledger. Finalize TEM-specific framing at Gate F.
