# Systematic Corpus Sampling Plan

## Corpus layers
A. Reviews/frameworks: used to reconstruct prior organizing schemes and baselines.
B. Landmark studies: used for historical anchoring and citation chaining.
C. Systematic primary-study corpus: used for quantitative SCDA evaluation.

## Time strata
- pre-1995: logistics/integration precursors
- 1995-2004: SCM consolidation
- 2005-2014: globalization, risk, sustainability, analytics
- 2015-2019: Industry 4.0 and digital supply chains
- 2020-2023: disruption, resilience, digital twins, advanced AI
- 2024-2026: generative/agentic AI and emerging autonomous decision systems

## Disciplinary strata
Management/strategy; operations management/OR; industrial engineering; information systems; computer science/AI.

## Topic strata
Planning/inventory; sourcing/procurement; production; logistics/transport; coordination/information; risk/resilience; sustainability/circularity; digitalization; analytics/AI; autonomous decision systems.

## Evidence strata
Conceptual; synthetic/simulation; benchmark; case/observational; pilot/deployed/longitudinal.

## Sampling principle
Do not optimize for a predetermined paper count. Continue retrieval and screening until each planned stratum has adequate representation and incremental searches/citation chaining cease to add materially new conceptual categories. Record saturation diagnostics.

## Minimum validation requirement
Before headline comparisons:
- every era represented;
- every discipline represented;
- every major topic represented;
- real empirical evidence represented alongside models/simulation;
- no single technology dominates the corpus;
- ambiguous coding retained for sensitivity analysis.
